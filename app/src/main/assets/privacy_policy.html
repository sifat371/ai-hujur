Al-Hujur AI - Privacy Policy

Application: com.aistudio.alhujurai.mkhzqp
Updated: September 2026
Contact: mdhasibulhasanofficial@gmail.com

1. Location Data

Location is used only to calculate local prayer times and Qibla compass bearing in real-time. It is not tracked in the background or shared with third parties.

2. Notifications

Local notifications alert you 10 minutes prior to prayer times and for daily morning/evening dhikr. Can be turned off in app settings.

3. Local Storage

Your prayer habits, daily amal, tasbeeh counts, and goals are stored exclusively on your device using a local Room SQLite database.

4. Scholar AI

Queries to Scholar AI are processed via the Google Gemini API to return Islamic references from Quran and Hadith. No personal data is attached.
