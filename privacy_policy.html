Welcome to Al-Hujur AI - Islamic Companion ("Al-Hujur AI", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding data collection, usage, and safeguarding when you use our Android application.

This policy is designed to comply with Google Play Developer Program policies, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Location Data (Foreground / Ephemeral Only)

Why we request location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION):

  • Accurate Prayer Times: Calculating exact prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and Sehri/Iftar timings based on astronomical calculation standards (Islamic Foundation Bangladesh, MWL, etc.).
  • Qibla Compass Direction: Calculating the accurate geographic bearing from your current position toward the Holy Kaaba in Mecca, Saudi Arabia.

Important Privacy Guarantees on Location:

  • Your location coordinates are processed ephemerally on your device.
  • We do NOT track your location continuously in the background.
  • We do NOT transmit, store, or log your GPS coordinates on remote external servers.
  • We do NOT share your location with advertisers or analytics brokers.

B. Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

With your explicit consent, Al-Hujur AI uses Android system notification channels solely for:

  • 10-Minute Advance Prayer Alerts: Notifying you 10 minutes prior to each prayer time so you can prepare for ablution (Wudu) and prayer.
  • Daily Spiritual Reminders: Morning/evening Dhikr reminders and Ramadan Sehri wakeup alerts.

You can customize or disable these alerts at any time directly in the app settings or via Android system settings.

C. Scholar AI Queries (Google Gemini API)

When you consult the Scholar AI assistant with everyday Islamic queries:

  • The query text is sent securely via HTTPS to the Google Gemini API solely for natural language processing and retrieving verified Quranic verses and Sahih Hadith citations.
  • No personally identifiable information (such as your phone number, real name, or location) is appended to AI requests.
  • AI queries are not linked to your identity for profiling or behavioral tracking.

D. Personal Spiritual Data & Amal Logs (Local Only)

Your daily spiritual records—including five-time prayer checkboxes, Quran recitation progress, digital Tasbeeh counts, and Ramadan fasting streaks—are stored in an encrypted/private SQLite database using Android Room directly in your device's isolated application sandbox.

  • This data never leaves your device.
  • We do not operate central user profile tracking servers.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver reliable Islamic features, we interface with trusted platform services:

  • Google Play Services: Required for Android location provider APIs and app integrity.
  • Google AI / Gemini API: Utilized server-side/securely to generate contextual Islamic knowledge and answers.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, lease, or distribute your personal information. We may only disclose data if required by applicable law or to protect user safety and security against malicious abuse.

5. Data Security

We implement modern technical and organizational safeguards:

  • All network interactions (such as Islamic knowledge retrieval) occur over encrypted Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Device-stored records reside within Android's sandboxed local storage, inaccessible to other third-party apps.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your prayer tracking and profile goals are saved locally on your phone, you have complete control over your data:

  • Reset Data: You can reset or edit your profile name and spiritual goals at any time in the app's Profile section.
  • Permanent Deletion: Clearing the app's data in Android Settings > Apps > Al-Hujur AI > Storage > Clear Storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local databases and cached information.
